4. Live Tracking & WhatsApp Notifications

Upon courier pickup from our Surat facility, a live tracking Airway Bill (AWB) number and direct carrier tracking link are automatically dispatched to your WhatsApp mobile number and SMS. You can monitor doorstep transit in real-time.

5. Transit Insurance & Damaged Delivery Guarantee

All parcels are 100% insured against loss, theft, or courier transit damage. In the rare event a parcel arrives with a compromised or broken outer seal, patrons are advised to refuse delivery and immediately alert our concierge at +91 90542 41725 for an immediate replacement dispatch.
