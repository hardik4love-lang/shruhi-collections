Privacy Policy

Shruhi Collections Surat • Statutory Compliance • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Shruhi Collections (www.shruhicollections.in), accessible from any web or mobile terminal, protecting our patrons' personal data and shopping privacy is our foremost priority. This Privacy Policy documents the types of personal information collected, how it is secured, and our strict non-disclosure commitments.

2. Information We Collect

When you browse our haute couture catalog, request size advice via our Curvy Sizing Engine, or initiate a WhatsApp concierge reservation, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Delivery Shipping Address, Pincode, and Email.
  • Sizing & Fit Preferences: Bust, waist, and hip circumference measurements shared for custom fit grading (Sizes S to 6XL).
  • Transaction Verification: UPI Reference Numbers (UTR), Transaction IDs, and payment screenshots submitted for order fulfillment. We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers or bank passwords.
  • Technical Log Data: Device IP addresses, browser types, and standard analytics cookies to optimize Core Web Vitals and load speeds.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized strictly to fulfill your bespoke orders:

  • To process, pack, and dispatch your ethnic ensembles directly from our Surat loomed facility.
  • To transmit courier tracking numbers and out-for-delivery alerts via WhatsApp and SMS.
  • To provide personalized curvy sizing recommendations through our dedicated stylists.
  • To comply with statutory legal and tax obligations under the Government of India Goods & Services Tax (GST) rules.

4. Zero Third-Party Data Selling

We enforce a strict Zero Data Selling Guarantee. Shruhi Collections never sells, rents, trades, or licenses your personal contact numbers, addresses, or order history to third-party telemarketers, brokers, or data brokers.

5. Courier & Payment Gateway Security

Information is shared only with verified fulfillment partners necessary to execute your delivery:

  • Logistics Partners: Delhivery, Blue Dart, and DTDC receive your name, address, and mobile number solely for door-to-door transit and delivery OTP verification.
  • Payment Processing: All transactions are executed via 256-bit SSL encrypted channels under RBI-regulated banking rails (Unified Payments Interface / UPI).

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), you retain full rights to inspect, update, or request the immediate deletion of your personal contact records from our concierge databases. To request record removal, email privacy@shruhicollections.in or message our Grievance Desk at +91 90542 41725.
